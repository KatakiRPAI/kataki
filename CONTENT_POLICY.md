# Content policy

Kataki RPAI is a local tool. It ships no models and no content, runs on your machine, and sends your text only to the model endpoints you configure.

- The project is intended for adults.
- Do not use it to create sexual content involving minors, or content depicting real people without their consent. Contributions (code, example characters, scenarios, assets) containing either will be rejected.
- You are responsible for the models and providers you connect and for complying with their terms and your local law.
- Example content bundled with the project stays suitable for a general audience.
