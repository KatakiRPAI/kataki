## 1. What this is for

A reader should be able to tell, without being told, that the woman behind the bar in chapter one
is the woman on the road in chapter nine. Everything here serves that.

The enemy is drift. Generate a picture from the last picture and the face moves a little each
time; by the tenth scene she is someone else. So **every picture of a character is made from that
character's frozen sheet, never from the previous scene**. The sheet is made once, looked at, and
kept. What changes between scenes — a cut lip, a borrowed coat, rain in her hair — is an overlay
written in words, not a new sheet.

The second enemy is a machine that cannot do it. Text and image models do not fit in 8 GB
together, so anything that loads a model takes **the lease** (§3.5) and gives it back. And the
default path makes no model call at all: a sprite from the character's pack, composited over the
place's plate. A library with no image model connected is not a broken library — it is the one
most people will have.

**Rules that hold everywhere in M3**

1. **Never generate from a previous generation.** A character picture is made from their sheet; a
   place variant is made from the master plate. Edit chains compound drift.
2. **A picture is a cache, never a source.** Every picture can be deleted and remade from the
   sheet, the plate, the style card and the scene. Nothing a picture holds is the only copy of
   anything.
3. **No model is required.** Every screen that shows a picture shows something good without one:
   today's procedural art (`app/src/art.tsx`'s `Portrait`, `Room`, `Figure`) stays as the floor.
4. **One model on the card at a time.** §3.5.
5. **Nothing is generated behind your back.** The engine never asks a model for a picture that
   nobody asked for, except from the idle queue, which only runs when you have turned it on.
6. **No new dependencies**, engine or app. Compositing is `blobs` + the browser; no Pillow, no
   diffusers, no InsightFace. Where a tier needs one (face verify), M3 builds the seam and stops.

## 3. The model

### 3.1 A character's sheet

One row per library character, in a new `looks` table. It holds:

- **`descriptor`** — the character's appearance as an ordered list of short clauses, always in the
  same order: build, age, skin, hair, eyes, face, clothing, bearing. Fixed order matters: the same
  words in the same places give the same picture back. Written by the utility model from the
  character's description and private card, or by hand.
- **`sheet_media_id`** — the frozen reference picture. Made once. Every later picture of them is
  made from this.
- **`pack`** — a sprite/expression pack: `{expression: media_name}` for neutral, smiling, wary,
  surprised, doubtful (the five the design brief names), plus whatever else is brought in. A card
  import that carried more than one picture fills this.
- **`locked`** — once true, the sheet and descriptor do not change without being unlocked. A
  character you have played ten scenes with must not silently become someone else.

### 3.2 A place's plate

One row per library place: a **master plate** and up to four variants keyed by
`art.timeOfDay`: dawn, day, dusk, night. Variants are made from the master, never from each
other. A place with only a master shows the master at every hour, tinted the way `Room` tints
today.

### 3.3 A story's look

One row per story: a **style card** — a handful of words that every picture in that story is made
with ("muted film still, harbour light, grain"), plus a **seed**. Two stories with the same
characters can look completely different; one story looks like itself.

### 3.4 The scene a picture is of

When a picture is wanted, the harness — not the model — builds the prompt from:
the story's style card, the place's plate for the current `timeOfDay`, and up to **three**
characters present, each as `sheet + descriptor + state overlay`. Anyone beyond three is not
drawn (the design brief: "at most three are drawn; anyone beyond that sits in a row of avatars").

The **state overlay** is where the story leaks into the picture: what the memory reader already
records as a character's flags (`holding`, `wearing`, `injury`) plus the expression the last line
suggests. The utility model turns the recent lines into a small JSON object against a closed
schema — the same discipline `extract.py` already uses — and the harness templates it. The model
never writes the prompt.

### 3.5 The lease

One asyncio lock in the engine. Anything that loads a model onto the graphics card takes it,
does its work, and gives it back; a local text model is asked to free its weights first where the
backend supports it (`llama-server`'s `/free`), and is left alone where it does not. A cloud
backend takes no lease. **A turn always wins**: if a reply is being generated, a picture waits.
`ponytail:` one global lock, because one card is the case; per-device scheduling only if someone
turns up with two.

### 3.6 The cache

A picture is keyed by the hash of everything that decided it: style card and seed, place plate and
hour, and each character's sheet, descriptor and overlay. The same scene twice is one picture and
one model call. Change a coat and it is a different key, and a different picture.

## 4. The UI

M2's screens were built from M1's vocabulary; M3's are built from M2's, and there are still no
mockups beyond the design brief's words.

- **The stage** (task 12): the place's plate for the hour fills the window behind the
  conversation, and whoever is on stage is drawn over it — the speaker lit, the others softened,
  at most three. With reduced motion it is still images and crossfades. Today's `Room` and
  `Figure` remain the floor, and a library with no pictures looks exactly as it does now.
- **A friend's look** (task 13): the profile grows a card — the sheet, the descriptor in its
  fixed order, the expression pack, and the lock. "Make a sheet" where there is none.
- **A place's plates** (task 14): Places shows the master and its four hours, and offers to make
  the ones that are missing.
- **Connecting an image model** (task 15): Settings → Models grows the `image` job, with the
  backends of §5 as presets, and says plainly that nothing needs one.
- **Asking for a picture** (task 16): a scene can be drawn on demand from the story menu, and
  the idle queue (off by default) draws the scene you are in while you read.

## 5. Backends

One interface, `images.py`, with a `draw(spec) -> bytes` per backend and nothing else:

1. **The pack** (task 9) — no model. A sprite from the character's pack composited over the
   place's plate, in the browser. This is the default, and it is the only one that works
   everywhere.
2. **OpenAI-compatible images** (task 8) — `/v1/images/generations` and `/v1/images/edits`,
   the same hand-rolled client discipline as `llm.py`. Covers fal, HF, and anything wearing that
   shape.
3. **ComfyUI** (task 10) — a workflow template with named slots the harness fills, posted to
   `/prompt` and read back from `/history`. Ships two templates: one that makes a sheet, one that
   makes a scene from a sheet.
4. **AI Horde** — the same interface, deferred past M3 unless tasks 8 and 10 land early.

Face-embedding verification (≤ 2 rerolls) needs a model M3 will not add. Task 11 builds the seam
— a `verify(sheet, made) -> float | None` that returns `None` when nothing can check — and the
reroll loop around it, so the tier can land later without moving anything else.

## 7. Amendment (2026-09-23): the HuggingFace slice

Approved by the user on 2026-09-23 in a design grilling. The work behind it is in
`docs/images/findings.md`: the three "via HF" sections. The local diffusers pipeline in `docs/images/`
stays a spike. It is not an app backend in this slice.

### 7.1 What changes

- **Backend: HuggingFace Inference Providers**, a fourth entry in §5. It is plain HTTPS through
  `router.huggingface.co/<provider>/…` using `httpx2`, the same way `llm.py` works, so **rule 6
  (no new dependencies) still holds**. `huggingface_hub` and Pillow stay out of the engine. The key is the one
  the Models page already keeps in the keychain for the HuggingFace provider.
- **Three models, one `image` job.** The job's model is the text-to-image model. Its params
  hold the other two, with these defaults:
  | Use | Model | Provider (fallback) | Cost |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | backgrounds, first drafts | `Tongyi-MAI/Z-Image-Turbo` | wavespeed (fal) | $0.005 |
  | expressions from the sheet | `Qwen/Qwen-Image-Edit-2511` | wavespeed (fal) | $0.03 |
  | cutout to transparent | `briaai/RMBG-2.0` | fal | $0.018 |
  The provider's own model id comes from the Hub's `inferenceProviderMapping` and is cached per process.
- **The sheet is `data.portrait`, and the pack is `data.pack`.** There's no `looks` table yet. A character's
  item already carries `portrait` (uploaded or drafted). The pack is
  `{"from": <portrait media>, "sprites": {expression: <media>}}` with the five expressions
  neutral, smiling, wary, surprised, doubtful, each a transparent PNG. When `from` is no longer the
  portrait, the pack is stale. It still shows, and the profile says so.
- **A place's background is `data.image`.** `Room` already shows it, tinted by the hour. It's one
  picture per place, with no hour variants (§3.2's variants wait for Pass A).
- **Style:** backgrounds are prompted as *realistic fantasy illustration*. Characters keep the
  model's own style. There's no per-story style card yet (§3.3 waits).
- **Expression on stage:** after a reply from a character who has a pack, the utility job picks one of
  the five against a closed schema (~$0.0002, before the stream closes). It is saved on the message in a new
  `messages.expression` column, **migration 8**. The stage shows each drawn character's sprite for
  the expression of their latest line (neutral if none). Characters without a pack show exactly what
  they show today.
- **No lease:** a cloud backend takes none (§3.5). **No cache table:** every picture is a click.

### 7.2 Rules this slice adds

1. **Every picture costs money, so every picture is a click.** Nothing retries on its own. That
   includes a refusal, which offers the other provider as a one-click retry (H5).
2. **Adults only.** Every character prompt says the character is an adult. A character whose own
   description gives an age under 18 or calls them a child is not drawn, and the app says why.
   NSFW and gore are otherwise allowed. What a provider refuses is the provider's call.
3. **The pack is made from the sheet, never from a sprite** (rule 1 of §1, unchanged).

### 7.3 Tasks

Each task is one commit. Verify with the engine checks and the standard app check from §6, plus the
real-call check named in the task. Real calls count against the user's **$0.50 test budget**. Spend
is reported at each task. Stop and ask before going over.

**H1. The HF image client.** `images.py`: `draw(prompt, w, h)`, `edit(sheet, prompt)`,
`cutout(png)`, each returning bytes or raising `ImageError(message, refused: bool, alt: str | None)`.
Wavespeed shape: `images: [...]`, `size: "W*H"`, polling `data.urls.get`. Fal shape: `image_url(s)`,
`image_size`, a queue poll. Both go through the router. Verify: tests against a scripted fake transport;
one real `draw` ($0.005).

**H2. A place's background.** `POST /items/{id}/draw` for a place → a realistic-fantasy prompt from
its name and description, 1536×864, saved as `data.image`. The image job appears on the Models page
with a HuggingFace preset. The Places page gets *Draw background* and *Draw again*. Verify: the Gull
gets a background, and the stage in a chat shows it tinted by the hour (~$0.01).

**H3. A character's look.** `POST /items/{id}/draw` for a character → a first draft from their
description on a plain background, 768×1024, saved as `data.portrait`. It can be redrawn.
`POST /items/{id}/look` → the pack: five edits of the portrait on a flat background, each cut out,
and `POST /items/{id}/look/{expression}` redraws one. The profile gets *Draw them*,
*Use this look* and the pack with per-sprite redraws. The adults-only guard lives here.
Verify: one demo character, drafted and packed (~$0.25).

**H4. On stage, with a face that fits the line.** Migration 8, the expression pick after a
reply, and `Figure` showing the pack sprite. Verify: a short chat through HF (Qwen3.5-9B on
every text job, thinking on for Reasoning) where the speaker's face changes with the line (~$0.02).

**H5. When a provider says no, and docs.** Refused or failed pictures show the provider's reason
and *Try on <other provider>*. README "Connecting a model" gains images, and `docs/images/README.md`
records the app wiring. Verify: a forced refusal against the fake shows the retry, and walk.
