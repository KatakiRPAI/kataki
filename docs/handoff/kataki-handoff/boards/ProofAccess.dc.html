Keyboard, focus, motion, contrast

Nothing in Kataki needs a mouse. These are the rules every board above follows; KEYBOARD.md and ACCESSIBILITY in the spec have the full lists.

Focus ring

:focus-visible only. 2 px of the ground colour, then 2 px of accent (--focus-ring). In a story the accent is the scene primary. Never removed, never clipped: containers that scroll get 4 px of inner padding.

In a story, focus lands in the composer. F6 cycles header → story → composer → widgets. Esc closes the top overlay and returns focus to what opened it.

High contrast

Appearance › High contrast raises every border and hairline, lifts muted text to near-ink, and dims the sky to a quarter. Colours of meaning (warm, ok, bad) don’t change. Windows high-contrast mode (forced-colors) is respected on top.
